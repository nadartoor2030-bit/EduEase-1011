# EduEase – Stress Management & Study Companion

EduEase is an AI-powered web application that combines education and mental well-being into one platform. It is designed to help students study more effectively while also managing stress through interactive wellness tools.

Instead of treating academics and mental health as separate problems, Mochi provides both learning assistance and self-care features in one place.

---

#Features

## Study Hub

The Study Hub provides multiple AI-powered tools to make learning easier and less overwhelming.

### Ask AI
- Ask questions on any topic.
- Get explanations in simple language.
- Upload notes or images for AI-assisted learning.

### Quiz Generator
- Generate quizzes from any topic.
- Practice concepts instantly.
- Helpful for revision before exams.
- Can specify number of questions and difficulty.

### Learning games
- AI generates educational games from your chosen topic.
- Makes studying more engaging and interactive.

### Mind Map Generator
- Enter any concept or topic.
- Automatically creates a structured mind map.
- Helps visualize relationships between ideas.

### Task Breaker
Students often receive long assignments that feel overwhelming.

Simply upload or paste an assignment and AI will:
- Break it into manageable chunks
- Estimate study time
- Insert relaxation breaks between tasks
- Make large assignments feel achievable

### Study Tips
Provides useful productivity and learning strategies for students.

### Dyslexia Support
A unique accessibility feature.

Users can click on **any word** on the website and it will automatically split into **phonetic syllables**, making reading easier for users with dyslexia.

# Wellness Hub

- Notice the good bits

- Managing academic workload
